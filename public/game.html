    Phone wheel

    Scan the code with your phone's camera (iPhone or Android) and hold the phone sideways like a steering wheel. Tilt it to steer; slide your thumbs up the left (brake) and right (gas) edges.

    Waiting for a phone

    Over Wi-Fi (same network)
    —
    The phone warns about the certificate once: tap Advanced, then Proceed. The gyro needs https.
    Over USB cable
    —
    Turn on USB debugging on the phone and plug it in. The server runs adb reverse for you.
